1. About the app

This app helps you select options and assign participants to results using Amida ladders and roulette. It does not supply user-entered prizes, transfer money or accept or manage bets.

2. Draws and manual editing

Automatically generated draws randomize prize positions or assignments and retain the assignment after starting. Manually editing ladder rungs or following a visible board to choose a starting position can affect the outcome. Check the entries and edits with participants before playing.

3. Participant and prize slots

Unequal lists are padded to the larger count using empty participant or no-prize slots. Repeated names or prizes are treated as separate entries.

4. Entries, sharing and participation

You control the content you enter and share. Check people's wishes before sharing their personal information. Preset challenges are optional; allow everyone to decline or choose a different activity.

7. Ad removal

Ad removal is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Draws, themes, audio and result cards remain free. Buying does not affect draw probabilities or results. The purchase page and store confirmation show your price and currency. The Japanese base price is JPY 480; regional or store prices may differ. Check the store confirmation before buying.

Use “Restore purchases” with the purchasing account on the same store. Transfers between iOS and Android are not provided. Deleting local data does not cancel your entitlement. Network conditions may delay verification. Refunds and cancellations follow the store's procedures; ads may resume if the entitlement is revoked. Applicable consumer rights are not restricted.
