Questions & answers

Is it free?

Draws, team splits and result cards are free. The app has ads, with an optional one-time purchase to remove them.

How many entries can I add?

Each participant or result list holds up to 50 entries, with up to 80 characters per entry.

Can I turn off the sound?

Set music, sound effects and vibration separately. The app also respects your device’s reduced motion setting.

Can I share results?

Copy results as text or share a result card as an image.
