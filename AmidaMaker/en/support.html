Using the app

How do I get started?

Enter participants and prizes/results, open the board, check it and start. Bulk input uses one entry per line. Each list supports up to 50 entries, with 80 characters per entry.

Can the participant and prize lists have different lengths?

The larger list sets the number of slots. Missing results become no-prize slots; missing participants become empty slots. To award the same prize to several people, enter it several times.

Can I choose who goes next or trace from the prize side?

Choose everyone at once or one at a time. In sequential play, choose any unfinished starting name. Tap an icon beside an input name to choose a unique character from Animals, Food, Nature, Space or Play. It moves along the path and stays visible in the overview. Between arrivals, use Start everyone remaining together to run all unfinished paths at once. Participants are shown as icons, with a one-line name when the board has six columns or fewer. Long names are shortened; tap the icon or name to read the full name. Flip the board to trace from the prize side; the assignment remains the same.

How do I change when results are revealed?

Show results from the start, on arrival, or on tap. With tap-to-reveal, open arrived boxes before continuing. The optional three-second countdown starts after revealing. Pre-start assignment preview is an optional setting.

How are assignments decided? Can the board change?

Automatic generation shuffles prize positions while retaining your original input order. Following visible paths to choose a starting position can affect the outcome. Explain this choice to participants. The draw screen does not offer rung editing. The board and reveal rules are locked after starting.

How are Pair and Pick one roulette different?

Pair mode assigns every participant on the inner ring to a result on the outer ring. Pick-one mode selects one entry from either list. Both lists are shared with Amida.

Short labels follow the curve; long labels or many entries use numbers. Tap a ring entry to read it, or start from the center or bottom button. The top-right list also shows every entry. Both methods open a draw screen after board confirmation and can be switched before starting or after results are complete.

What happens if I leave the app during a draw?

Sound, language and entries are saved on the device. Leaving the app pauses the animation; use Resume draw after returning. After an app restart, the saved assignment is retained and an unfinished animation can replay from the beginning. Returning to the input lists ends that draw.

How do result cards and sound settings work?

Open a result card to edit its title and review hiding participant names before sharing. The title and prize text remain visible. Control music, effects and vibration independently using the sound button. Vibration strength depends on the device.

How do I reuse saved lists or draw history?

Use From history beside Participants or Results to review and import a previous list. Replace either list independently and undo the change. Result presets include Win / Miss and Group split. Settings also lets you save named lists together with their draw rules.

How do Avatar Race and Space Jump work?

Switch between overview and enlarged views for large groups. Tap a participant icon to see the name, rank and result. Results are assigned in input order by finishing rank.

Can I change the number of Amida crossbars?

Choose Low, Normal, High or Extreme before starting. Normal has slightly more rungs; Extreme makes a much more tangled route. Density does not change the draw duration.

How long does a draw take?

At normal speed, simultaneous draws finish in about seven seconds. One-at-a-time Amida keeps its existing 5.5-second base per person, adjusted by the speed setting. The result reveal adds about one to three seconds.

Purchases & ads

What is free, and what does ad removal include?

Amida, roulette, themes, audio, saved lists and result cards are free. The free version displays banners on the input screen and may occasionally show a full-screen ad when returning to input after reviewing results. Ad removal removes both formats. Ad removal is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Buying it does not change draw probabilities or results.

The Japanese base price is JPY 480. Prices may differ by region or store. Check the price and currency on the store confirmation before buying.

I already paid. How do I restore ad removal?

Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account used to buy, connect to the internet, then open Settings → Remove ads → Restore purchases. If payment is pending, complete the store's process before trying again.

Purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android. Deleting local app data does not cancel your entitlement. If restoration fails, contact us with the store, purchase date and information identifying the order. Do not send passwords or card numbers.

How do I request a refund?

See the refund guidance for the store where you purchased. Eligibility depends on store conditions and applicable law. You can also contact us if you need help with a purchase.

Data, privacy & troubleshooting

How do I delete saved data?

Use Settings → Delete all saved data to delete entries, saved lists and draw progress. Your display language, purchase entitlement, ad consent and shared images are kept.

Manage images already shared, OS backups and store transaction records separately. See the privacy policy for details.

Are usage analytics and crash reports sent?

For app quality and stability, production builds automatically enable usage analytics and diagnostics through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. There is no in-app reporting toggle. Reporting is separate from advertising consent; analytics advertising-ID collection and advertising-related use are disabled.

Participant names, prize/result text and shared images are not added to our custom events or diagnostic attributes. Analytics includes ad load success/failure, impressions, clicks and revenue information provided by the ad SDK. Deleting local saved data does not delete information already sent.

Ad privacy choices appears in Settings when available. Change iOS tracking permission in system settings. Firebase Remote Config retrieves operational settings such as disabling ads. See the privacy policy for the data types and external services involved.

Which devices and languages are supported? Do I need an account?

For phones and tablets running Android 7.0 (API 24) or later, or iOS 15 or later. Supports Japanese, English, Korean, Traditional and Simplified Chinese, Thai, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Brazilian Portuguese, Malay and French. No dedicated app account registration is required. Purchases and restoration use your store account.

Sound, vibration or another feature is not working

Check music, sound effects and vibration under Sound & vibration in the app. Also check device volume, silent settings, audio output and vibration support. Haptic features and strength vary by device.

If the issue continues, send your device model, OS/app version, steps to reproduce, expected behavior and what happened. Contact us before deleting data or reinstalling if you want to keep saved entries.
