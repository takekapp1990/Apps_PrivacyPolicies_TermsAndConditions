Flag Memory AwaPop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy defines the handling of user information sent from the application service "Flag Memory AwaPop" (hereinafter referred to as "this app") provided by takekapp1990 (hereinafter referred to as "we"), and applies to all customers (hereinafter referred to as "users") who use this app.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using this app.

By using the app, users are deemed to have agreed to this Privacy Policy.

Article 1 (User Information Items Sent from This App)

(1) User Information Automatically Obtained in This App

We obtain information about the user's application usage and device information (OS information, etc.) when the user uses this app.

We do not require your real name, but process identifiers for users within the app. Do not include personal information in posts or inquiries.

On-device learning records and the current ranking feature

Learning history, country records, game results and personal bests are saved on your device and do not automatically sync to other devices. They are separate from purchase restoration. Public rankings are disabled in the current version: it does not send new public scores or profiles or display rankings. Existing records sent by earlier versions are not automatically deleted. The ranking descriptions below explain those earlier versions and retained records.

Legacy user statistics for rankings

Earlier versions collected and stored the following information for rankings.

  • User ID (device ID)
  • Display name (player name)
  • Avatar image URL
  • User level, experience points, number of avatars owned
  • Last update date and time
  • Language code, country code, platform information

These existing records are stored in Firestore and were used for rankings, game progress and category filters in earlier versions. Uninstalling does not delete server data. Contact us below about deletion.

Legacy game result data for rankings

Earlier versions collected and stored the following information for rankings.

  • Game result ID
  • User ID (device ID)
  • Score
  • Game settings information (game mode, category, difficulty, etc.)
  • Play date and time
  • Other game statistics

These existing records are stored in Firestore and were used for monthly and overall rankings and performance comparisons in earlier versions. There is currently no automatic deletion period.

Usage Data (Analytics)

This app collects and stores daily and monthly usage data (app usage status, frequency of feature usage, etc.) to improve the app and develop features.

This information is stored in Firestore and is used for app improvement and feature development, and usage analysis. It is retained permanently (for aggregation and analysis).

Device Information

This app processes device identifiers and platform information to provide features and identify users. Earlier versions also stored these for rankings. The iOS vendor identifier and Android device identifier are separate from advertising identifiers and Country Stories anonymous authentication IDs.

This information is stored in local storage and Firestore, and is used for user identification, ranking data management, and app feature provision. Uninstalling does not delete server data. Contact us below about deletion.

(2) Contact Information Regarding This App

When users make inquiries about this app, we may ask for their email address and other information related to the inquiry as necessary.

(3) Permissions Used

Local Folder Access (path_provider)

This app accesses the app's sandbox (iOS) or internal storage (Android) to save app data (settings, game data, etc.) and temporary files.

We only access the app's sandbox and do not access other apps' or users' files. No additional permission settings are required.

iOS tracking permission and advertising consent

On iOS, the app checks App Tracking Transparency (ATT) status before initializing advertising SDKs or requesting ads. If the status is undetermined and the OS permits a request, it displays the permission prompt. Ads do not start while the status remains unresolved. Learning remains available if permission is denied or restricted, and ad requests use a non-personalized setting.

ATT permission is separate from advertising consent managed by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app presents required UMP messages and checks whether ads can be requested before starting ads. ATT approval alone does not mean every user receives personalized ads: applicable consent, region, age protection and provider settings still apply. When required, the app’s advertising privacy options let you review your UMP choices. ATT permission can be changed in device settings.

Information for Usage Analysis

This app uses Firebase (Google Inc.) as a tool to collect app usage data for future development reference, and Firebase may automatically obtain user information. For information about what information is obtained, the purpose of use, and provision to third parties, please check the Google Privacy Policy from the link below.

Google Privacy Policy
https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en

On iOS, Firebase Analytics collection is disabled by default and is enabled for usage analysis after the ATT status check completes, including when permission is denied or restricted. Its consent defaults for ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are disabled and remain disabled in this startup process. ATT approval is not treated as consent for Firebase Analytics advertising use. Firestore usage aggregates and Firebase Crashlytics for diagnosing failures are separate features.

Country stories and in-app feedback (added October 19, 2026)

Country Stories and Share Your Country store your self-reported country or territory of origin, topic, text, optional nickname, optional age range and optional YouTube video URL in association with a Firebase anonymous authentication user ID. Origin is self-reported, not a measurement of your location; GPS is not used. Age range is optional. Do not submit real names, addresses or contact details.

Only text submitted after agreement to the posting rules, translation and publication is sent to Google Cloud Translation for translation into the ten supported languages: Japanese, English, Spanish, German, Korean, Thai, Indonesian, French, Brazilian Portuguese and Traditional Chinese (Taiwan). Nicknames, age ranges, video URLs and authentication IDs are not sent for translation. Legacy posts may retain the original seven-language translations and are displayed using an available translation. After operator review, the original, translations, origin, topic, optional video URL, optional nickname and publication date may be shown to other users. A nickname is optional and is reviewed before publication; posts without one are displayed anonymously. Age range and authentication IDs remain private. Do not put your own or anyone else’s personal information in text or nicknames.

YouTube video URLs are stored. When a video card is displayed, the app sends the normalized URL to YouTube’s oEmbed service to retrieve its title and thumbnail information. Thumbnail images also load from YouTube’s servers, which may process IP addresses and other information involved in those requests. The app does not collect or store the videos themselves or embed video playback. Playback opens in an external app or browser. Preview requests and external playback are subject to YouTube’s Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Reports store the post identifier, revision, reason and time privately with the reporter’s anonymous authentication ID. Reported posts are temporarily hidden for operator review. In the current version, hiding stores the post’s public identifier and revision privately with your anonymous authentication ID to hide that revision on your own screen. It does not block the poster: other posts or later revisions may appear. The poster is not notified. Show the post again from Hidden Posts on the board. Legacy poster-block settings migrate to hiding the then-current published revision, while compatibility with older clients is retained. Posting suspension records are stored privately to prevent abuse.

Country information corrections and Feedback / Bug Reports store category, message, relevant country or section, app version/build, OS, device model, platform and interface language with an anonymous authentication ID, request identifier and submission time. Device model means the model name; this form does not request advertising IDs, hardware identifiers, your name or email address. Reports are private to the operator and used for investigation, corrections, service improvement and abuse prevention. The in-app form does not provide individual replies.

Retention, deletion and contact

Use Delete My Response in Share Your Country to delete your submitted original, nickname, age range, video URL, translations and published copy. An online connection is needed to complete deletion. Drafts are stored on your device. Showing a hidden post again removes its hide setting. If reinstalling or other changes replace your anonymous authentication identity, you may lose access to manage earlier posts and hide settings in the app.

Uninstalling does not delete server data. There is currently no automatic deletion period for undeleted submissions, reports, suspension records, feedback, rate-limit records or usage aggregates. Request deletion through Feedback in the drawer or the contact email below. We may ask for information needed to identify the relevant data and respond subject to records needed for legal obligations or abuse prevention. Deleting a post does not automatically delete reports or submitted feedback.

These features use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check and Google Cloud Translation. The database is in a United States region, and processing may occur outside your country. Only authorized operators can review private responses and reports. These features do not use story or report content for advertising or advertising tracking. Existing advertising, analytics and purchase processing through AdMob, Firebase, RevenueCat and other services described in this policy continue to apply separately.
