This Privacy Policy defines the handling of user information sent from the application service "AwaPop" (hereinafter referred to as "this app") provided by takekapp1990 (hereinafter referred to as "we"), and applies to all customers (hereinafter referred to as "users") who use this app.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using this app.

By using the app, users are deemed to have agreed to this Privacy Policy.

Article 1 (User Information Items Sent from This App)

(1) User Information Automatically Obtained in This App

We obtain information about the user's application usage and device information (OS information, etc.) when the user uses this app.

Basic gameplay does not require registration of a legal name or contact details. Display names, feedback messages and selected images may contain personal information provided by the user.

Records and settings stored on your device

Training history, records used for analysis, earned badges, favorites and settings such as notification preferences are stored on your device. Automatic history and badge synchronization between devices is not provided. Changing devices or deleting the app may cause local data to be lost. Game analysis describes in-game performance; it is not a medical diagnosis or a measurement of cognitive ability.

Feedback and bug reports sent from the app

When you choose to send a report, we store its category and message, anonymous authentication user ID, submission time, app version and build number, OS and device model, and display language in Firebase Firestore. We use this information to review feedback, investigate problems and improve the app. The diagnostic context does not include game history, advertising IDs, device-owner names or hardware identifiers. Please do not include personal or confidential information in the message.

Anonymous authentication and online rooms (2.0.0 and later)

Online play and feedback use an anonymous user ID issued by Firebase Authentication. Users do not register an email address or password. We store authentication and membership information, display names and icons, and in-game answers and scores in Firebase to run multiplayer games and control access.

A public lobby listing shows its host's display name and icon, participant count and similar lobby information before joining. Display names, in-game player identifiers, answers and scores are shared with participants in the room. Authentication user IDs and join passwords are not included in the public lobby listing.

Rooms stop accepting participation and reads no later than 24 hours after creation, and also close when the host ends or leaves the room. Cleanup processes remove room content and shared images after closure or expiry. Access-control identifiers are normally retained for about seven days after expiry and then become eligible for automatic deletion. Network or server conditions can delay deletion.

Anonymous authentication records are managed separately from room content. Uninstalling the app does not immediately delete cloud authentication records. An expired anonymous session may be renewed before joining online play.

Custom images

Images you select and add, along with names you assign to them, are stored on your device. Adding an image for solo play does not upload it to the cloud. You can remove these local images in the app or by uninstalling the app.

Images used online are converted for sharing and stored on the server. They are retrieved by users authenticated as current room participants. The conversion removes attached metadata such as location information. The local cache of shared images is held in memory and discarded when leaving the room. Server copies become subject to the cleanup process described above after closure or expiry.

Legacy online migration period

We plan to close the legacy online protocol after 2.0.0 becomes available. During migration, legacy room reads and changes and shared-image access use different restrictions from the new version, and people outside the room may be able to access them. Do not include personal or confidential information in names or images shared through the legacy protocol.

Rankings and previous cloud records

This version does not provide public rankings or automatic cross-device synchronization of game history and badges. Display names and ranking records uploaded by previous versions are not deleted from the cloud simply by uninstalling the app. Contact us about information stored in the cloud.

Usage Data (Analytics)

This app collects and stores daily and monthly usage data (app usage status, frequency of feature usage, etc.) to improve the app and develop features.

This information is stored in Firestore and is used for app improvement and feature development, and usage analysis. It is retained permanently (for aggregation and analysis).

Device information and identifiers

For app operation, support, abuse prevention and usage analysis, we process information such as OS, device model and app version, together with identifiers issued by the app or its service providers. Storage and retention differ according to the purpose and service involved.

(2) Contact Information Regarding This App

When users make inquiries about this app, we may ask for their email address and other information related to the inquiry as necessary.

(3) Permissions Used

Selecting photos

Adding custom images uses the operating system's photo picker or photo access permission appropriate for the OS version. The app uses the images selected by the user. Choosing online sharing uploads a converted copy as described above.

Local Folder Access (path_provider)

This app accesses the app's sandbox (iOS) or internal storage (Android) to save app data (settings, game data, etc.) and temporary files.

We only access the app's sandbox and do not access other apps' or users' files. No additional permission settings are required.

(4) User Information Obtained by External Services

Advertising and advertising SDKs

The app includes Google AdMob and Unity Ads SDKs for advertising. These services may process device or app identifiers, approximate location, ad impressions and interactions, app usage, performance and diagnostic information, and purchase-related information. Uses include ad delivery and measurement, analytics, service quality and fraud prevention, and may involve sharing with the providers and other advertising parties. Processing varies with app version, ad configuration, features, region and privacy choices.

When an advertising privacy-settings entry is available in the app, you can review or change the available choices there. You can also use advertising-identifier and privacy controls offered by your operating system. Refer to each provider's policy for details, including retention and deletion.

You can view current profile information, records and badges in the app. Contact us about records stored in the cloud.

We implement the following security measures to protect user information.
