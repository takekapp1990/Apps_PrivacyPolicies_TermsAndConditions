FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about gameplay, records, and optional Premium features.

What can I enjoy for free, and what is included in Premium?

Core games, record viewing (including the 5-area radar chart and weekly changes), all 50 badges, standard characters, and the Character Collection are free to enjoy. Optional Premium (an auto-renewing subscription) adds deeper result analysis (28-day comparisons under matching settings, mistake and response trends, time-of-day comparisons, and practice suggestions), additional characters, and ad-free play.

Do charts and analysis appear right away?

Your daily play records are saved right away. Trend comparisons and detailed analysis appear once enough comparable records have been collected—just start with a game or two from the "Today" tab.

Do my records automatically sync across devices?

No. Records are stored locally on your current device, and automatic cross-device sync is not supported.

Does the app measure cognitive ability or provide medical diagnosis?

No. The records and charts shown in the app are solely for reviewing your in-game performance and do not provide medical diagnosis, cognitive ability measurement, or guaranteed ability improvement.

Can I still play the classic Awa Awa Pon name-memory game and multiplayer?

Yes! The classic name-memory mode, online multiplayer with friends and family, and custom character photos and nicknames are all still available.

Are character names generated by AI on the spot during gameplay?

No. Character names are not generated on the fly by AI during gameplay; they are chosen from curated preset lists designed for fun sound and memorability (and you can also add your own custom names and images).
