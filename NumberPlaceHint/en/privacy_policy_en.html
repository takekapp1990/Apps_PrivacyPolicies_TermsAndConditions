"Sudoku Hint" Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the information collected from the application service "Sudoku Hint" (hereinafter referred to as "the App") provided by TakeKApp (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us") and how it is handled. This policy applies to all customers (hereinafter referred to as "Users") who use the App.

Users should read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App.

By using the App, Users are deemed to have agreed to this Privacy Policy.

Article 1 (Items of User Information Collected by the App)

(1) User information automatically obtained within the App

We obtain information related to the User's use of the application and device information (OS information, etc.) when the User uses the App.

We do not collect information that personally identifies Users when they use the App.

(2) Puzzle Analysis Data

For the purpose of improving the App, enhancing OCR recognition accuracy, and collecting statistical information, we may transmit and store Sudoku puzzle data (such as grid number placement) read by the User within the App to our server. This data does not contain any personally identifiable information.

(3) Inquiry Information regarding the App

When a User makes an inquiry regarding the App, we may request the User to provide their email address and other information related to the inquiry as necessary.

Optional replay publication and reactions

Replays are shared only when you explicitly choose to publish each recording and confirm what will be shared. We send the puzzle grid, operations, candidate notes, edits, relative elapsed times, hint usage, the solve date (without the time), difficulty and visual presentation settings and show them to other users for 30 days. Original photos, names, free-text notes and exact play start/end timestamps are not published. Anonymous authentication identifiers, publication management records, likes, blocks and reports are stored to operate the feature. You can stop publication or request deletion from My published replays or shared data settings. Further delivery stops after publication ends; copies already received cannot be fully recalled. Bodies and reactions are deleted in subsequent processing, and ended publication records after 30 days. Reports and investigation records are kept as needed to handle them. The app manages ownership through anonymous authentication; you do not need to save IDs or codes. If anonymous credentials are lost, contact support to request removal.
