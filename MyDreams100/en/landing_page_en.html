Before a hundred, start with one.

A list you’ll want
to look at again.

A quiet café, a book you meant to read, someone you’d like to see. Write things as they come to you and choose cards you enjoy. Each time you open your list, you can pick something to try next.

01 / Find it

Find your next
“I’d like to try that.”

You don’t need a hundred ideas at once. Answer a question or explore ready-made ideas, then put what matters to you into your own words.

“If you had unlimited time and money, what would you do?”

Keep your interests, values and thoughts about the future in “My future self.”

02 / Make it yours

Dress your list
in your favorite designs.

A simple list one day, a sticker journal the next. Combine card designs, wallpapers and theme colors to give your list its own character.

These three designs require a one-time purchase or an active Premium subscription. Free basic designs are also available.

03 / Imagine what’s ahead

Picture the person
you’d like to become.

How would you like to spend your days a few years from now? Keep the thoughts you have today, even before you know the answer. Add a photo or an illustration from the app and revisit it later.

Your “My future self” answers are saved on your device and are not automatically shared.

04 / Look back

Remember more
than a checked box.

Add photos and notes to your achievements. The monthly album brings together what you did and what you recorded. Small steps along the way belong there, too.

When a little inspiration would help

Share the joy
of “I did it.”

Explore the monthly topic or find inspiration in other people’s wishes. Share the achievements you choose, congratulate someone, or add an idea to your own list.

Sharing is optional. You can use your list and reflections entirely on your own.

Before you begin

Do I have to write a hundred things straight away?

Start with one. Questions and ready-made ideas can help, and you can add more whenever something comes to mind.

Can other people see what I write?

Your personal list is not automatically published. You choose which cards to share with the community.

What if I change phones?

Export your records using the backup feature and restore them later. This is not automatic syncing between devices. Restore purchases through the store where you bought them.
