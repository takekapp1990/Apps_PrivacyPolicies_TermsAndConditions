My Dreams 100 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy defines the handling of user information sent from the application service "My Dreams 100" (hereinafter referred to as "this app") provided by TakeKApp (hereinafter referred to as "we"), and applies to all customers (hereinafter referred to as "users") who use this app.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using this app.

This policy explains how information is handled. Where consent or device permission is required by law or platform features, we ask for the applicable choice or permission separately.

Article 1 (User Information Items Sent from This App)

(1) User Information Automatically Obtained in This App

We obtain information about the user's application usage and device information (OS information, etc.) when the user uses this app.

The app processes user IDs, device identifiers and other information needed to provide its services. An email address supplied for support, or information included in posts or public profiles, may also identify an individual.

(2) User Information Obtained Through Social Features

When using the social features of this app, we collect the following information:

  • Display name, age, country/region, avatar, color and visibility settings entered or selected for a profile
  • User IDs from services such as Firebase anonymous authentication and information used to verify ownership of profiles and posts
  • Wishes or achievement records selected for publication, attached photos and text, and posting dates
  • Likes, preset congratulatory messages and other reactions, including the reacting user and the relevant post
  • Report information (information about which user reported which post)

You choose which profile fields to display. Age can be hidden, shown as an age range, or shown exactly; location can be hidden, shown as country only, or include a region. Other users can view the posts, photos and profile fields you choose to publish. Avoid including contact details or other private information in public content. Age, country and region depend on user input or device settings and are not guaranteed to be accurate.

Personal records, backups and notifications

Personal lists, notes, photos, reflections and future-self images are managed on the device. Content you choose to publish in the community is sent to Firebase databases and storage. Image sharing, text export and file saving send the selected content to the destination you choose. Device or cloud backups are also subject to the settings of those services.

Password-protected backups are created as encrypted files on the device. Take care when storing or sharing files and passwords. When notifications are used, notification settings, push-notification device tokens, language and user IDs may be used to deliver and manage notifications.

(3) Contact Information Regarding This App

When users make inquiries about this app, we may ask for their email address and other information related to the inquiry as necessary.

(4) User Information Obtained by External Services

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob for banner ads and optional rewarded video ads. Our mediation configuration includes Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), and InMobi Exchange. Actual providers depend on the app version, OS, region, privacy choices, and each provider’s approval and delivery settings. Not all providers serve ads at all times.

Advertising services may process the following, depending on the provider, app version, device settings, and your privacy choices:

  • Advertising IDs, including Android advertising ID and iOS IDFA when permitted, and other device or app identifiers
  • Device model, OS, language, app/advertising SDK versions, IP address, and connection information
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks, video viewing/completion, and consent/privacy settings
  • Approximate country or city inferred from IP addresses or similar information

Uses include ad delivery, frequency management, performance/revenue measurement, fraud prevention, and service maintenance/improvement. See each provider's official policy for processing, sharing, retention, and privacy rights:

  • Google Advertising Policy
  • Unity Ads: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle): Privacy Policy
  • InMobi Exchange: Privacy Policy
Advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent messages where required. When available, open “About ads” in the app menu, then “Ad privacy options” to review choices. Available options vary by region and circumstances.

On iOS, cross-app tracking and IDFA access are subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) restrictions and permission. Manage applicable permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android also provides advertising ID controls in device settings. Non-personalized ads may still involve information processing for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Article 2 (Purpose of Use)

We will use the information collected from users, including user information, device information, and other information related to the use of this app, for the purpose of operating, developing, and improving this app, responding to inquiries, improving services, and developing new products, and will handle it appropriately in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information obtained through social features is used for the following purposes:

  • Bucket list post content: Display to other users, provision of like and report features
  • Age, region, country name, and icon information: Display of user profiles
  • Like information: Provision of like features and creation of statistical information
  • Report information: Management of inappropriate content and provision of automatic filtering features

Article 2.2 (Data Retention Period for Social Features)

Published posts and photos are displayed according to their visibility settings and remain on the server until deletion is completed. Unpublishing content does not delete the server-side data. Uninstalling the app or changing devices does not necessarily delete published content or server-side information. Profile, reaction and report records are retained for the periods needed to provide features, address misuse and investigate support requests.

To request deletion of server-side information, or if you can no longer use the original device, contact the support address in Article 5. We verify the relevant records and ownership before acting. Information needed for legal obligations, misuse investigations, or operational backups may not be erased immediately. Delete local records and any backup files you saved or shared from their respective storage locations as well.

Article 3 (Prohibition of Disclosure/Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)

We will properly manage the personal information entrusted to us by customers and will not disclose personal information to third parties except in the following cases.

  • When the customer has given consent, or when service providers process information as needed to provide the features, advertising, analytics or purchases described in this policy
  • When disclosure is required by law

Article 4 (Change of Purpose of Use)

We will change the purpose of use of personal information only when it is reasonably recognized that the purpose of use after the change is related to the purpose of use before the change.

When the purpose of use is changed, we will notify users of the changed purpose by our prescribed method or publish it on this website.
