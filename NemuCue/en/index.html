How to use · Night Screen

Set your alarm.
Wait with a dim screen.

Adjust the brightness and clock display in Night Screen. You can also hide the display while waiting and tap to see the clock again.

  1. 01

    Choose a time

    Use a duration such as 30 minutes, or set the time you want to wake up.

  2. 02

    Wait with Night Screen

    Dim the screen. Turn on relaxing sounds or light only if you want them.

  3. 03

    Finish when you are up

    Complete the stop tasks and finish the remaining reminders you no longer need.

On iPhone, keep the screen unlocked.

Keep Night Screen open to use custom vibrations. Locking with the side button or switching apps puts vibration under iOS control; your chosen pattern and strength will not apply. A black Night Screen is not the same as locking your phone.

Repeat on the days you choose.

Set a time and weekdays for recurring alarms, such as every day or workdays.

FAQ

Before you start.

Can I lock my iPhone while using the app?+

Keep Night Screen open to use the app’s custom vibration patterns. While your phone is locked or another app is open, iOS controls vibration and your chosen pattern and strength will not apply.

Does the app play sound?+

The alarm is designed to use vibration instead of an alarm tone. Relaxing sounds play only when you turn them on. Vibration can make noise against a desk or bedding, and device settings can affect behavior. Test it where you plan to use it.

What can I use for free?+

Four vibration modes, standard multiple reminders, stop and snooze tasks, Night Screen, relaxing sounds and light. Adjusting reminder count and spacing, editing stop tasks, creating patterns and removing ads require Premium.

Do I need an account?+

No app-specific account is required. Settings and schedules are stored on your device. See the privacy policy for information about data used for advertising, analytics and purchases.

What should I check before using it?+

Follow the app’s introduction to allow the required permissions, then try Permissions & Test. Android battery-saving settings may affect operation. Alarm delivery and waking are not guaranteed.
