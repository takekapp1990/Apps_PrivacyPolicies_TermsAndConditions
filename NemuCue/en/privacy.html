Nap & Stay Awake

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

TakeKApp ("we") provides Nap & Stay Awake, an app that signals scheduled times through vibration. This policy explains information used by the app and its advertising, purchase and usage analytics services.

1. Alarms and settings stay on your device

No Nap & Stay Awake account is required. Alarm names, display messages, weekdays and times, vibration patterns, dismiss and snooze steps, and other settings are stored locally. Information needed to schedule an alarm is also passed to the device operating system. We do not send alarm-related input such as alarm names, messages, selected times, custom vibration waveform data or challenge answers. On Android, if you enter a provided access code, the code is sent to RevenueCat to verify premium access. It is not recorded in usage logs. Fixed feature categories, such as vibration modes, dismiss methods and relaxation sound types, and their usage counts may be sent as usage data described below.

Alarm names and messages may appear on the lock screen, depending on device settings. Operating system backups and restores may be subject to the settings and policies of Apple, Google or another OS provider.

3. Your choices

  • You can allow or stop usage counters and diagnostic uploads under Settings → About the app → Usage data. An explicit opt-out from a previous version is also preserved. Stopping collection does not automatically erase previously sent information.
  • When additional advertising privacy choices are required, you can review them through the advertising privacy setting. Availability and choices depend on your region and consent status.
  • You can review or change tracking permission under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can use the app without allowing tracking.
  • You can change alarm and notification permissions in device settings. Related features may not work without permission.

Analytics, Firestore counters and Crashlytics uploads follow the same usage data choice. Where consent is required, at most 64 action events are held only in memory before a decision and sent if you allow collection. Unsent events are discarded on refusal or app exit. Stopping collection prevents new counter uploads, discards queued action events and requests deletion of unsent Crashlytics reports. Requests already in flight may not be cancellable. Advertising and purchase processing follow their separate choices and purposes.

4. Device permissions

The app uses OS features and permissions for scheduled notifications, alarm display including lock screen presentation, vibration, background alarm operation, restoring schedules after restart, and preventing automatic lock while the Night screen is open. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location access.

5. Support information

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any device, OS or purchase information you choose to include to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, card numbers or unnecessary personal information.

6. Retention, deletion and international processing

You can manage local schedules and settings by editing or deleting them or removing the app. Also check any OS schedules and backups. Firestore counters retain counts per date and anonymous installation so that we can produce daily and monthly usage reports through administrator tools. Contact us below about deletion requests for submitted counters and diagnostics. Information already sent to external services is retained according to their retention settings and policies, the needs of purchase restoration and fraud prevention, and applicable law. Services such as Google, RevenueCat and Apple may process information outside your country.

The app does not offer a user-facing account with a name, email address or sign-in screen. It uses the anonymous authentication identifier described above to protect access to usage counters. Contact us below about access or deletion requests. Identity verification and identification of the relevant data may be necessary. Some information, including store transaction records, cannot be deleted by us.

7. Children

Nap & Stay Awake is a general audience utility and is not designed primarily for children. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child, we will investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.
